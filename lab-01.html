Lab 1

Connect to the Virtual Lab Environment

Use CloudShare to access your assigned lab virtual machines, then sign in to the Experience Center.

Time: ~10 minutesTasks: 3Outcome: Lab + console access verified
i
Use your own session values

Each learner has a unique tenant and CloudShare link. In the sample access email, Student ID zs000578 maps to student@zs000578.labtenant.com. Replace all examples with the values from your Lab Access Details.

1.1

Verify CloudShare Lab Access

Open your assigned CloudShare environment and locate the virtual machines.

  1. Open the CloudShare lab URL provided for your session. Sign in with the email and passphrase from your invitation or classroom instructions.
  2. On the Overview page, select Go to My VM List. Allow the lab VMs to finish provisioning, then open the Corp Client PC tab.
  3. Use the Lab Guide tab for lab materials and the left-side Credentials panel for your assigned Zscaler tenant and SDC details. Do not substitute example credentials for session-specific accounts.
✓
Expected result

You can open the CloudShare workspace and access the assigned Corp Client PC and session credential panel.

1.2

Use the Lab Clipboard

Transfer text from your computer into the CloudShare virtual machine.

  1. Copy the command, URL, or value from this guide. In the CloudShare VM controls, open Actions → Send Text.
  2. Paste the text into the Send Text field and select Send. Use the VM’s paste action if prompted.
✓
Expected result

You can enter commands and values into the virtual machine without retyping long strings.

1.3

Sign In to Experience Center

Open the administrator portal from the Corp Client PC.

  1. Open Corp Client PC in CloudShare. Under Actions, use Send Password to unlock Windows if required. The local VM username is student.
  2. In its browser, open https://console.zscaler.com.
  3. Sign in as student@<Student FQDN> using your session-specific password from CloudShare Credentials. The Student FQDN is the domain assigned to your pod, not the literal text inside angle brackets.
  4. Explore the Experience Center landing page and its Search Menu. Use search when menu locations differ from the lab screenshots.
✓
Expected result

The assigned administrator can sign in to Experience Center and locate features through the Search Menu.

✓
Lab 1 complete

Pod, clipboard, and Zscaler console access are verified.
