Lab 1

Connect to the Virtual Lab Environment

Verify the Skytap pod, clipboard, and Zscaler Admin Console access.

Time: ~10 minutesTasks: 3Outcome: Lab + console access verified
i
Use your own session values

Each learner has a unique tenant and Skytap link. In the sample access email, Student ID zs000578 maps to student@zs000578.labtenant.com. Replace all examples with the values from your Lab Access Details.

1.1

Verify Lab Access

Start the pod and confirm the required VMs are running.

  1. Open your unique Skytap Portal URL from the Lab Access Details email. The link is valid for the duration of your session.
  2. Select all VMs and click Play if needed.
  3. Wait until all three show Running. Startup is staged and may take a few minutes.
✓
Expected result

Corp: App Connector, Corp: Client PC, and Corp: Win Svr 2016 are running.

1.2

Use the Lab Clipboard

Copy text from your computer into the Windows VM.

  1. Click Clipboard in the Skytap toolbar.
  2. Paste your text into Copy/paste.
  3. In Windows, use Ctrl+V or right-click → Paste.
⌘
Using a Mac?

Inside the Windows VM use Ctrl+V, not Cmd+V.

1.3

Sign In to the Zscaler Admin Console

Sign in to the client and verify admin-console access.

  1. Open Corp: Client PC.
  2. From the Skytap toolbar: Ctrl-Alt-Del → Credentials → Insert Credentials for student.
  1. Open the Zscaler Admin Console.
    https://console.zscaler.com/
  2. Sign in with your student admin account.
    • Username: student@<Student FQDN>
    • Password: use the session password from your Lab Access Details email. Although the email template may call it a one-time password, it remains valid for the session unless you are prompted to change it.
  3. If the portal prompts for a password change, complete it and use the new password for the remainder of the session.
✓
Expected result

The Zscaler Admin Console / Experience Center loads successfully.

✓
Lab 1 complete

Pod, clipboard, and Zscaler console access are verified.
