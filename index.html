Session password

The access email may label this value One Time Password. For this lab environment, treat it as your session password: it remains valid for the training session unless you are explicitly prompted to change it.

VM

Your Skytap pod

Each participant also receives a unique Skytap link. It gives you access to the virtual machines used in the hands-on labs and expires with the session.

  • Corp: Client PC — learner workstation
  • Corp: Win Svr 2016 — directory, DNS, and internal services
  • Corp: App Connector — ZPA connector VM

Keep the original access email available during the course; it is the authoritative source for your assigned URL, tenant identity, and session password.

Labs 14–16

Solutions Demo Center is a separate read-only environment

Do not use your personal lab tenant for the final three labs.

What changes at Lab 14

Labs 14, 15, and 16 use a separate, pre-configured Solutions Demo Center (SDC) tenant. Learners do not build the configuration there; the goal is to view, verify, and investigate existing data and settings.

The SDC credentials are included in the same access email. In most tasks you only need the Admin Username and Password. The first sign-in may also require 2FA enrollment.

Why the detailed SDC steps are not here

The full SDC sign-in instructions are intentionally placed immediately before Lab 14 so learners see them when they are needed, instead of having to remember them from the beginning of the course.
