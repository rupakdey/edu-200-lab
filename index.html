Your session

Access your lab tenant through CloudShare

Use the CloudShare environment assigned for your training session. Once signed in, open Credentials to find your Zscaler tenant and SDC accounts. No separate lab-credentials email is sent.

!
CloudShare requires your corporate email address

Sign in with your corporate/work email address. Free personal email accounts such as Gmail or Yahoo are not accepted. After login, select Credentials in CloudShare for your Zscaler tenant and SDC login details.

Labs 14–16

Solutions Demo Center is a separate read-only environment

Do not use your personal lab tenant for the final three labs.

What changes at Lab 14

Labs 14, 15, and 16 use a separate, pre-configured Solutions Demo Center (SDC) tenant. Learners do not build the configuration there; the goal is to view, verify, and investigate existing data and settings.

Find the SDC credentials under CloudShare → Credentials. For Labs 14–16, you generally use the SDC Admin Username and Password; the first sign-in may also require 2FA enrollment.

Why the detailed SDC steps are not here

The full SDC sign-in instructions are intentionally placed immediately before Lab 14 so learners see them when they are needed, instead of having to remember them from the beginning of the course.
