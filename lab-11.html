Lab 11

Troubleshoot Access to an Internal Application for a Specific User

Diagnose why Joe cannot reach the intranet and restore access by assigning ZPA.

Time: ~25 minutesTasks: 2Outcome: Missing ZPA entitlement found and corrected
i
Use your assigned tenant

Use the Student ID, Student FQDN, session password, and Skytap link from your own lab-access email. Values shown in examples and screenshots are not your live credentials.

11.1

Identify the Root Cause

Reproduce Joe’s failure, then inspect his end-user service entitlements.

Current Experience Center navigation
Administration›Identity›Users
  1. On Corp: Client PC sign in to ZCC as joe@<Student FQDN> using the password created in Lab 2. Browse to http://intranet.patraining.safemarch.com and confirm access fails.
  2. Open
    Administration›Identity›Users
    Find Joe, open the user, and review Entitlements.
  3. Confirm Joe has ZIA through Helpdesk L1 but does not have the ZPA/Private Access service entitlement.
✓
Expected result

The root cause is identified: Joe is missing the ZPA service entitlement.

11.2

Resolve the Access Issue

Assign ZPA to Joe, refresh the endpoint policy, and retest the intranet.

Current Experience Center navigation
Administration›Entitlements›End User Entitlements›Service Entitlements
  1. Open
    Administration›Entitlements›End User Entitlements›Service Entitlements
    Select Zscaler Private Access → Users → Assign Users → select Joe → Next → Assign.
  2. Verify Joe now receives the Private Access/ZPA service entitlement.
  3. On Corp: Client PC open ZCC and choose Update Policy. Confirm Private Access becomes visible/active.
  4. Browse again to http://intranet.patraining.safemarch.com and confirm access succeeds.
✓
Expected result

Joe now has ZPA service access and can reach the intranet.

✓
Lab 11 complete

Missing ZPA entitlement found and corrected
