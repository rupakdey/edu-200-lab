Lab 9

Provision ZPA Infrastructure

Create an App Connector Group, enroll the lab connector with an OAuth token, and verify healthy connectivity.

Time: ~25 minutesTasks: 2Outcome: App Connector registered and healthy
i
Use your assigned tenant

Use the Student ID, Student FQDN, session password, and CloudShare link from your own lab-access email. Values shown in examples and screenshots are not your live credentials.

9.1

Create the App Connector Group

Prepare the logical group required for OAuth-based App Connector enrollment.

Experience Center · find this resource
Infrastructure›Private Access›Component›App Connector Groups

Search Menu → App Connector Groups

Official documentation: Configuring App Connector Groups

  1. In Experience Center, search for App Connector Groups and select Add.
  2. Enter the settings below, then save the group.
    SettingValue
    NameOnPrem
    StatusEnabled
    Enrollment CertificateConnector
    App Connector LocationYour nearest appropriate city
    Other settingsLeave defaults unless the exercise specifies otherwise
  3. If prompted about the maintenance window, acknowledge the warning. Verify that the OnPrem group appears in the list.
✓
Expected result

The enabled OnPrem App Connector Group exists and is ready to receive a connector.

9.2

Enroll and Activate the App Connector

Regenerate a lab OAuth token and register the connector with ZPA.

Experience Center · find this resource
Infrastructure›Private Access›Component›App Connectors

Search Menu → App Connectors

Official documentation: Configuring App Connectors

  1. Open the Corp ZPA Connector VM console. Sign in with the lab VM credentials.
    Usernameadmin
    PasswordZscaler1!
  2. In the connector console, stop the service and regenerate its lab-only OAuth token using the following commands in order:
    sudo systemctl stop zpa-connector
    sudo su
    rm -rf /opt/zscaler/var/*
    sudo systemctl start zpa-connector
    cat /etc/issue
    !
    Lab VM only

    The removal command resets local connector enrollment material. Run it only on the assigned training connector, not a production App Connector.

  3. In Experience Center, search for App Connectors and choose Add. Use these settings:
    App Connector GroupOnPrem
    Enrollment TokenNew token shown in the VM console (session-specific)
    Save. If the token has expired, regenerate it in the lab VM and retry.
  4. Refresh the connector list and wait for Connection Health: Connected. Open its details to verify Host OS, location, IP address, and uptime. Allow a short status-update interval.
✓
Expected result

The App Connector is associated with OnPrem, its health changes to Connected, and connector details are available.

✓
Lab 9 complete

OnPrem is configured and the OAuth-enrolled App Connector reports Connected.
